Does a law library help a small AI model?

Name:     Date:  

A research project gave small AI models old multiple-choice questions from the US bar exam, the exam that people take to become lawyers. Sometimes the model could also read a passage from a law library. Before the main runs, the project's plan fixed what counted as success. This is not legal advice, and the scores are not bar exam scores.

Part A. Predict

Do this before you look at any result. For each claim, circle what you expect.

  1. A model scores higher with a law library than with nothing to read. It heldIt did not
  2. A model scores higher with the exact passage that a law student marked than with the library. It heldIt did not
  3. Searching only the right state's laws works better than searching every state's laws. It heldIt did not
  4. If a model reads only a court decision that was later overruled, it picks the outdated rule more often. It heldIt did not
  5. Taking overruled decisions out of the search results fixes that problem. It heldIt did not
