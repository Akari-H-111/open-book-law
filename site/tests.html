The 11 tests

Before the main runs, the project wrote down 11 claims and how big an effect each one needed. Here is each claim, what was measured, and the verdict.

When does a claim count as supported? Three things must hold. The effect is at least as big as the plan required. The uncertainty range stays on one side of zero. And the claim passes a correction for running 11 tests.

Ten of the tests measure a difference. For these, the plan needed the measured difference (the dot) to reach the dashed line, and the range only has to stay away from zero. Test H9 measures a share. There, the low end of the range must be above the 30% line.

“Not supported” does not mean “proven false”. It means that this experiment did not show the effect clearly enough.

Classroom mode hides the verdicts and the charts. Ask students to guess for each claim whether it held. Then press “Reveal the verdicts”.
