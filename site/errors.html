Why answers went wrong

The project took 100 random questions that the model got wrong while it read from the encyclopedia. An AI model then gave each wrong answer one reason.

These reasons are an AI first pass. The author has not reviewed them yet. They describe only questions that the model got wrong, so they do not show how often search fails overall. The reasons use legal terms, and they are shown as the AI wrote them.
